## What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are — and are
**not** — used across **{{ site.company.legal_name }}**'s **KidsStudio** apps
(the "**Apps**") and this website, [www.azulize.com]({{ site.url }}) (the
"**Site**").

## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website can store in your browser. Cookies
are widely used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and — the kind we
avoid — to track people across the web for advertising.

## Cookies in our Apps

**Our Apps do not use cookies.** They are native mobile apps, not web pages.
They also contain **no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics**. A small
amount of information (such as game progress and settings) is saved locally on
your device to make the App work; it never leaves the device and is not used to
track you. See our
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/legal/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details.

## Cookies on this website

This Site is a simple, static information page. **We do not set any advertising,
marketing, or analytics cookies**, and we do not embed third-party trackers,
social media pixels, or ad networks.

The Site is hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.). To deliver and secure the
page, GitHub's servers may process basic technical information such as your IP
address, and in some cases may set a strictly-necessary cookie for security or
load-balancing. These are essential to serving the page and are not used by us
to profile you. For more information, see GitHub's
[Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and [Cookies documentation](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-subprocessors-and-cookies).

We do not load web fonts, videos, maps, or other third-party embeds that would
set additional cookies.

## Managing cookies

Because we set no tracking cookies of our own, there is nothing to opt out of on
our side. If you'd still like to control cookies generally, you can manage or
delete them through your browser settings. The pages below explain how:

- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/help/4027947)

Blocking cookies will not break this Site, as it does not rely on them.

## Changes to this policy

If our use of cookies ever changes, we will update this page and revise the
