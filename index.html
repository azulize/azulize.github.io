Why Azulize

Built for kids. Designed to earn a parent's trust.

🛡️

Kid-safe by design

No ads, no pop-ups, no links out to the web without a parental gate. What a child sees is only ever the game.

🔒

No accounts, no profiles

We don't ask for names, emails or logins, and we build no profile of your child. Game progress stays on the device.

📵

No trackers or analytics

No third-party SDKs quietly watching. No advertising identifiers. No profiles built about your child.

👨‍👩‍👧

Made with parents

Purchases and settings live behind a parental gate. You stay in control of spending and screen time.

🎯

One idea per app

Each app does a single thing well — calm, uncluttered and easy for small hands to understand on their own.

✈️

Works offline

No connection required. Perfect for car rides, flights and waiting rooms — no surprises, no interruptions.

About Azulize

A small studio with a simple belief.

Azulize LLC is a U.S.-based software studio. We make the kind of children's apps we'd happily hand our own kids: calm, beautiful and free of the tricks that fill so much of the App Store — no ads, no manipulative pop-ups, no data harvesting.

Our catalog is a family of eighteen apps, each focused on one gentle idea. Guiding every screen is Milo, our friendly mascot, cheering kids on as they color, connect, match and create.

We're a U.S. limited liability company built for the long haul — small by choice, careful by habit.
